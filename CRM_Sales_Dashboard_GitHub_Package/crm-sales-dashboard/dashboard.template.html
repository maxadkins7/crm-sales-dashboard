The data behind the dashboard

Metric definitions, joins, and interpretation

This dashboard uses the supplied Maven Analytics CRM Sales Opportunities sample files. It is a historical snapshot, with engagement dates in 2016–2017 and recorded closes from March through December 2017. These are sample business records, not Max Adkins’s employer or client data.

Won revenue

Sum of close_value for opportunities marked Won. Values are in USD. Account annual revenue is a separate firmographic field.

Win rate

Won ÷ (Won + Lost). Open opportunities are excluded from the denominator. A segment without closed deals displays a dash.

Open opportunities

Count of records in Engaging or Prospecting at the snapshot. No historical stage transitions or open-deal dollar amounts are supplied.

Average won deal

Won revenue ÷ number of Won opportunities. The metric describes deal records, not unit sales.

Average time to win

Mean calendar days from engage_date to close_date for Won deals with both dates. This is not time since opportunity creation.

Close period

Quarter selections filter by close_date and exclude undated open records. Full snapshot includes all records. The trend always groups won revenue by close month.

Data preparation

  • 8,800 unique opportunities, 85 accounts, 35 sales-team records, and 7 products. Accounts, products, and sales agents are joined by their name keys. Inactive team members without opportunities do not appear in rankings.
  • Product GTXPro is normalized to GTX Pro for the catalog join. Sector technolgy is displayed as Technology. Original CSVs are unchanged.
  • 1,425 opportunities have no account name. They remain included and are labeled “Unassigned account”; their sector is “Unassigned”. All nonempty account names match the account table.
  • 2,089 open records lack close_date and close_value. Of these, 500 Prospecting records also lack engage_date. Missing dates and values are displayed as dashes, never invented.
  • Account annual revenue is provided in millions of USD. Catalog prices are reference list prices, not recorded open-deal values. The dashboard does not estimate pipeline dollars, forecasts, or quotas.

Reading the visuals

The stage chart is a distribution of current records, not a conversion funnel. Product and team rankings use recorded won revenue. Monthly change compares the latest two months in the selected period; absent records count as zero, and change is unavailable when the earlier month is zero. All metrics respond to the global filters. Opportunity search and stage selection apply only to the opportunity view and its export.

Everything runs in your browser. The downloadable HTML contains the dataset and needs no server, account connection, external scripts, or internet access.
